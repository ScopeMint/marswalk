BEAT-BY-BEAT SCRIPT
===========================================================================

BEAT 1 — THE PROBLEM (0-6 s)
Screen: show JPL Mars Trek (global view), then cut to our app. Visual contrast.
Words on screen (subtitle): "Today's Mars maps are built for global views."
Then: "Astronauts will walk just a few kilometers."
Narration (optional voice-over or just subtitles):

  [point to the 6 km box on our app]

BEAT 2 — PICK THE TARGET (6-12 s)
Screen: click one of the science target cards in the left panel. Map flies to it.
Subtitle: "Pick a science target — boxwork formations at Mount Sharp, Gale Crater."
Show the target card opening, map centering on the target.

BEAT 3 — LAYERS ON (12-18 s)
Screen: toggle slope overlay on (see the terrain colors), then walk-back-time layer, then thermal.
Subtitle: "Layer five kinds of NASA mission data: topography, optical, thermal, radiation, temperature."
Show at least 3 layers toggling. The slope colors popping on is visually striking.
If thermal layer is not ready: show slope + walk-back-time + optical.

BEAT 4 — ROUTE DRAWS (18-24 s)
Screen: the route line appears (or was already there). Optionally drag the slope limit slider to show
the route detour or fail.
Subtitle: "A slope-aware route draws in under one second, avoiding terrain above the crew's limit."
Show the route line, the elevation profile below it, the walk-back-time number in the summary card.

BEAT 5 — SAFETY SUMMARY (24-30 s)
Screen: zoom into the summary card. Show the EVA budget bar, checklist items (green checks), the
radiation and temperature values.
Subtitle: "MarsWalk checks the full EVA: distance, time, radiation, temperature — with every number sourced."
END CARD (30-33 s): project name "MarsWalk", team name "Dark Matter Devs", team members' names,
"2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge — The Next Frontier"

===========================================================================
RECORDING TIPS
===========================================================================
- Use QuickTime on Mac: Cmd+Shift+5 -> Record Entire Screen or Selected Portion.
- Target resolution: 1920x1080 (Full HD). Make sure all text is readable.
- Do a 60-second PRACTICE RUN on Saturday night. Watch it back. Fix any choppy transitions.
- Keep the browser zoom at 100% (Cmd+0). The map looks best at full size.
- Close other apps before recording. The app should be fast and smooth.
- Record 3-4 takes. Use the best one.
- If the route takes >1 s to draw: pre-click the target before starting the recording, then
  just show it already drawn and move to the safety card.
